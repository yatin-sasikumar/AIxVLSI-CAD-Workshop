# AI × VLSI CAD Workshop

## Overview

The AI × VLSI CAD Workshop explores the intersection of **artificial intelligence, VLSI design, and electronic design automation (EDA)**.

The workshop focuses on understanding how modern hardware designs move through a tool-driven design flow and how AI can be integrated into that flow for debugging, analysis, optimization, and automation.

The central engineering loop is:

> **Problem → Algorithm → Tool → Measurement → Feedback → Improvement**

The emphasis is on practical implementation, measurable results, and reproducibility rather than treating AI as a black-box solution.

---

## Objectives

By the end of the workshop, participants should be able to:

- Understand the fundamentals of VLSI CAD and EDA workflows.
- Work with RTL designs and hardware verification tools.
- Understand how synthesis transforms RTL into hardware representations.
- Interpret EDA tool reports and identify design issues.
- Understand key design metrics such as area, timing, congestion, and wirelength.
- Apply algorithmic and AI-based approaches to EDA problems.
- Build simple tool-driven AI workflows.
- Evaluate AI-generated solutions using actual EDA tools.
- Distinguish between an **AI suggestion** and a **verified engineering result**.

---

## Workshop Structure

### Day 1 — VLSI CAD & EDA Foundations

Day 1 introduces the foundations required to work with AI-assisted EDA workflows.

Topics include:

- VLSI CAD algorithms
- Graph-based approaches
- Partitioning
- Floorplanning
- Placement
- Routing
- Timing analysis and closure
- RTL simulation and verification
- RTL synthesis
- EDA reports and metrics
- Tool-driven debugging
- AI × EDA workflows

Participants will work through a practical hardware design flow while observing how different tools provide different forms of engineering evidence.

### Day 2 — AI × EDA Challenges

Day 2 focuses on applying AI to practical EDA problems.

The challenge tracks include:

- **EDA Error Intelligence**
- **VLSI CAD Optimization**
- **RTL / SVA / UVM Assistant**
- **AI EDA Agent**

Each challenge emphasizes:

- Working implementation
- Tool integration
- Machine-readable outputs
- Fresh-machine reproducibility
- Measurable results
- Verification of AI-generated changes
